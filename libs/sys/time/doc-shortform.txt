monotonic(): number
  Seconds (nanosecond fractions) since an arbitrary point: never jumps
  (unlike os.time()), not CPU time (unlike os.clock()). For elapsed time

sleep(seconds)
  Waits seconds (with fractions; <= 0: not at all). Signals don't cut it
  short: a signal.on callback runs after it

sleep_until(deadline)
  Waits until monotonic() reaches deadline. For a steady rhythm without
  drift: next = next + interval each round
